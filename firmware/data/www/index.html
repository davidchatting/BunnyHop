Connection

Home Wi-Fi

On your home Wi-Fi, BunnyHop is at http://bunnyhop.local/, and the rabbit's own network becomes rabbit-only. Without home Wi-Fi, join the rabbit's network instead (it is hidden once a rabbit is paired, so add it by name) and this page opens by itself.

Rabbit

To pair, hold the rabbit's head button while plugging it in, until its lights are blue.
