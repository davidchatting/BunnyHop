BunnyHop setup

Install BunnyHop on an ESP32 and connect your Nabaztag:tag. The rabbit's own firmware is never touched.

This page talks to the ESP32 over USB, which needs Chrome or Edge on a computer.

  1. Connect the ESP32

    Plug the ESP32 (4 MB flash, e.g. Adafruit Feather ESP32) into this computer with a USB data cable, then press Connect and pick its port (e.g. CP2104).

    No port listed? Your board may need a USB-serial driver (CP210x for the Feather), or the cable may be charge-only.

  2. Install BunnyHop

    Copies BunnyHop's firmware and web app onto the ESP32. It takes about a minute.

  3. Pair the rabbit

    BunnyHop's Wi-Fi network is …

    Put the rabbit in setup mode: unplug it, hold the button on its head while plugging it back in, and let go when all its lights are blue. BunnyHop finds it by itself.

    Waiting for BunnyHop to start…

  4. Home Wi-Fi optional

    With home Wi-Fi, you reach BunnyHop at bunnyhop.local from any device at home, and its own network becomes private to the rabbit. Without it, you join BunnyHop's network to use it.
